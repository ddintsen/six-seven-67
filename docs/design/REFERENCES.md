## Owner clarification: playful Six Seven

The owner rejected the reserved first design and clarified that 67 refers to the playful "six seven" meme. This supersedes the original sober visual treatment while retaining the transaction and provenance requirements.

REF-7 [Gumroad](https://gumroad.com/), opened live at 1440×1000 and 390×844 by the reference scout: saturated color, confident grotesque, simple broad controls and original outlined characters coexist with legible product information. Adapt the humor, controlled color and character/product relationship. Do not copy pink, G-coins, brand illustrations, logo or marketing hero.

The numeric mascot is an original drawing. The recognizable palms-up gesture supplies the joke; 67 never becomes an invented probability. No source imagery is reused.

## Owner references, rechecked October 11, 2026

REF-3 [Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/) was reopened in installed Chrome at1440×1000 and390×844. The current public discovery page puts outcome rows and restrained prices alongside a bounded chart, with compact secondary navigation. Adopt information density, numeric alignment and question/action proximity. Keep67's own font, colors, content and geometry. Reference screenshots are temporary QA material in work/reference-v4; account-specific portfolio rows are not accessible without authentication and are not claimed as inspected.

REF-8 [Kalshi](https://kalshi.com/), explicitly named by the owner. Its public page and official [Portfolio: Balances and Positions](https://help.kalshi.com/en/articles/13823844-portfolio-balances-and-positions) and [Market FAQs](https://help.kalshi.com/en/articles/13823821-market-faqs) were read through web tools. The documented pattern distinguishes cash, quoted position value, costs and payouts, with state filters and separate activity. Adapt that distinction to practice credits and retain unresolved positions without a fabricated payout. Local Chrome could not resolve kalshi.com, www.kalshi.com or help.kalshi.com, so rendered Kalshi screenshots and authenticated portfolio visuals remain an explicit reference gap; no access restriction was bypassed.

Synthesis: compact working rows rather than oversized questions and paragraph stacks. Separate Activity from held positions. Avoid copying external identities, implied cross-chain liquidity, unsupported cash-out controls and showing potential payout as profit. The quality comparison for rendered density uses the opened Polymarket shots; Kalshi supplies verified portfolio mechanics.
